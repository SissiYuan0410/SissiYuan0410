Project 5: Week

Data Portrait

About This Project

My Data Portrait tracks how many times I said "I'm so hungry" each day for one week.

This habit reflects my daily mood and eating schedule. It also shows how my hunger changes depending on my classes, sleep, and stress. Each circle in my visualization represents one day of the week. The size of each circle shows how many times I said "I'm so hungry" that day — the bigger the circle, the hungrier I felt. The color of the circle goes from light brown to deep red, representing time from morning to night.

All the circles together look like an expanding and shrinking stomach, showing how my hunger grows and calms down throughout the week. I collected my data by writing down every time I said "I'm so hungry" for seven days, including when and where I said it.

By tracking how often I said "I'm so hungry," I realized that my hunger is not only about needing food, it also reflects my emotions and routines. I noticed I say it more when I am tired, anxious, or when I stay up late. Through visualizing this data, I gained a clearer sense of how my body and feelings interact with time and stress.
