Project 10: Time Delay Oracle

Behavior-Based Fortune-Telling Experience

About This Project

This project explored the relationship between impatience, emotion, and digital interaction through a fortune-telling experience made in p5.js. Instead of asking the player to type a question, I wanted the interaction to feel more personal and psychological, so the player would silently think about a question while interacting with the screen. The final result changed depending on how many times the player clicked during the waiting process.

Through this project, I became more interested in how user behavior can reveal emotional states. Fast and repeated clicking could represent anxiety or impatience, while slower interaction could feel calmer and more controlled. I also experimented with different visual stages and atmosphere changes to make the experience feel more immersive.
