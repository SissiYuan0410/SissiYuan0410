Project 9: Designed for Right Hands

Inclusive Interaction Experience

About This Project

This project made me think more deeply about how design can feel different for different people. I created a p5.js interaction based on left-handed and right-handed users, and I wanted to show how many everyday systems are unconsciously designed for right-handed people. Through different small tasks and interactions, the experience became easier or harder depending on the player's choice at the beginning.

During the process, I also learned more about balancing concept and interaction design. I spent time adjusting the controls, timing, and visual feedback so the project could feel more immersive and understandable. Even though the project was simple, it helped me realize that small design decisions can strongly affect user experience.
