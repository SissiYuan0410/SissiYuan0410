Project 6：Game

This is an autobiographical game based on my experience studying in a highly competitive high school in China. Instead of retelling memories directly, the game turns my past into interactive challenges—allowing players to feel, rather than simply read, what that period of my life was like. The first level uses a shooting mechanic: players face floating “questions,” representing academic pressure, expectations, and criticism that constantly appeared during that time. The interaction becomes symbolic—rather than avoiding these pressures, the player has to actively confront and break them. The second level shifts into a maze, reflecting the confusion and disorientation I felt about identity, future choices, and self-worth. There is no single “right” direction, and the uncertainty becomes part of the experience. Through simple mechanics and metaphorical interactions, the game transforms personal memories into a playable narrative about anxiety, expectation, resistance, and slowly finding a way forward.
