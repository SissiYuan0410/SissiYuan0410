Project 8: Attention Camera

Selective Recording System

About This Project

This project explores the idea that cameras are often seen as neutral tools that record everything equally, but in reality, perception is selective. I wanted to design a camera that does not treat the entire image the same, but instead prioritizes where attention is directed over time.

By using the mouse as a simulation of gaze, the system tracks where the user chooses to focus and only captures moments when attention is sustained. This shifts the role of the camera from passively recording reality to actively reflecting human perception.

The project suggests that what we "see" and what gets remembered is not the full visual field, but what we choose to attend to.
