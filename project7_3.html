Swim

Interactive Typography · Part 3

About This Project

The word "SWIM" comes alive, floating through gentle waves. Each letter wiggles independently as you guide the text across the canvas, creating a playful aquatic experience.

Controls: Use WASD keys to navigate. W moves up, S moves down, A moves left, and D moves right. Watch as the letters ripple and rotate with the movement.
