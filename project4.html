Project 4：Illusion

I researched optical illusions that use rhythm and repetition. I designed a grid that “breathes” by changing each square’s size with a sine wave. I used for loops to create the grid and if/else statements for alternating colors. After testing, I adjusted the speed and size so the motion feels calm and continuous.

This illusion could work as a browser extension to make waiting screens feel relaxing.

As a wearable, it could appear on clothing with LEDs that pulse gently with the wearer’s heartbeat.
