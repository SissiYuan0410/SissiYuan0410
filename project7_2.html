Logic

Interactive Typography · Part 2

About This Project

The word "LOGIC" becomes a puzzle—each position within the letters contains a hidden math problem. Hover to explore, click to select, and solve the arithmetic challenge.

Controls: Click on an expression to solve it. Type your answer and press Enter to submit. Press ESC to go back, or R to regenerate new problems.
