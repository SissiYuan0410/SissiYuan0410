Reflection

Through this creative coding course, I went from knowing nothing about p5.js to building interactive visuals and simple 3D experiments on my own. At first, programming felt dry and abstract, but the moment I created a shape that followed my cursor, I realized how playful code could be—more than text, it could become a visual language.

I learned how to combine HTML/CSS with p5.js, structure responsive pages, and debug issues like canvas scaling or interaction errors. These challenges helped me understand problem-solving as part of the design process rather than something separate from it.

In the future, I want to explore deeper 3D capabilities and possibly connect p5.js with A-Frame to build more immersive, interactive environments. This course taught me that creative coding is really about creativity plus code—technology is only a tool, imagination is the core.
