Project 3: Person

Interactive Experience

About This Project

His name is Channel-42.

He was once an ordinary office worker, endlessly stuck in a routine of paperwork and meetings. Over time, his head was replaced by a television, broadcasting not news, but his unfinished tasks and repressed emotions. His suit and tie still remind people of his human past, but his body has fused into the machinery of corporate life.

He roams the office on his rolling chair-wheels, carrying static and flickering signals wherever he goes.
